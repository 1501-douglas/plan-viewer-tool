What the viewer is for

The viewer shows the owner's design intent on one plan: layout, lighting and power, heating and cooling, and plumbing fixtures. It also lists the questions that are still open. In it you can:

  • Switch between the first floor and the lower level
  • Turn layers on and off, or pick one trade at a time
  • Zoom to a room
  • See the open items, split into resolve before submission and confirm later
  • See what is demolished, what is new and what moves, on the Existing / demo / new layer
  • Look at the twelve owner sheets: layouts, lighting and power, existing / demo / new (SC-1 to SC-3), and Owner E, Owner M and Owner P

It is not the permit set, an engineered design, or a material or finish schedule. It does not certify that anything is coordinated. It shows the owner's intent as of the date printed in it.
